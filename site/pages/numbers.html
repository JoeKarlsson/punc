Over time

Sentences got shorter and semicolons nearly vanished. Each dot is a book at the year it first came out, and the white line is the median for every 25 years. Translations count by the year of the original. The {{ancient}} books from before 1600, Homer and Plato among them, are off the left edge.

The white line is the median of every book.

Genres

Each genre's average fingerprint: every mark's share of all the marks, on the same scale as the fingerprints on the shelf. A book can sit in more than one genre.

Authors

Can the marks name the author?

Take one book away from its author. Average the fingerprints of each author's other books, and guess whichever author's average is closest to the book. For the {{authors}} authors with at least three different works on the shelf, that guess names the right author for {{hits}} of {{guessed}} books, {{hitRate}}. Guessing at random would be right about {{chance}} of the time.

Each square is one of an author's books, oldest first: pink where the guess named the author. Spread is how far an author's books stray from their own average fingerprint, where 100 is the gap between two books picked at random from the shelf. The lower it is, the more alike their books are.
